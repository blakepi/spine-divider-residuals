    ## Article requirements verified

    - Article main text: up to 4,500 words excluding abstract, Methods, references, and figure legends.
    - Abstract: up to 150 words, unreferenced.
    - Display items: 3-8 figures and/or tables.
    - Structure: Introduction without heading; Results with subheadings; Discussion without subheadings; Online Methods with topical subheadings.
    - Initial submission accepts PDF, Word, or TeX/LaTeX formats; TeX submissions should include compiled PDFs.
    - Manuscript must include author names/affiliations for unblinded submission, methods sufficient for replication, and reference list.
    - Cover letter should explain importance and fit for Nature Neuroscience and disclose related manuscripts/prior discussions if any.
    - Nature Portfolio requires availability statements for data/code/materials sufficient to verify claims.
    - Nature Portfolio AI policy requires LLM use beyond simple copyediting to be documented and prohibits generative-AI images for publication.
