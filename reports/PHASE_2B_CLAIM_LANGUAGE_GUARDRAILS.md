# Phase 2b Claim Language Guardrails

## 1. Allowed Claim Language

- Summary-level anchors occupy distinct load-normalized regimes.
- The result supports a load-normalization hypothesis for part of the high- versus low-isolation contrast.
- Popovic measured and Popovic model-fit rows were kept separate.
- Kwon and Cornejo remain context/exclusion rows without accepted `rho_L` coordinates.

## 2. Allowed-With-Caveat Language

- The analysis is not a per-spine reanalysis.
- Harnett remains high under fixed/recovered common loads, with the diagnostic 666.667 MOhm value acting as a boundary probe.
- Popovic measured remains low under fixed/recovered common loads, while diagnostic low-load probes can move it into intermediate but not high.
- Transfer proxies are proxy-only compatibility checks and are not SPINE residuals.

## 3. Forbidden Claim Language

- "we resolved the spine-isolation controversy"
- "per-spine published data validate SPINE"
- "Harnett measured spines were reanalyzed per spine"
- "external data validate the divider-residual model"
- "these rows estimate biological prevalence"
- "Kwon and Cornejo confirm the rho_L split"

## 4. Required Caveats

- Summary-level anchors only.
- No per-spine inference.
- Harnett is a model/summary anchor, not recovered paired measured per-spine data.
- Popovic measured summary and Popovic model-fit summary are separate rows.
- Kwon and Cornejo remain context/exclusion rows.
- Transfer proxies are not SPINE residuals.
- Common-load ratios equal `R_neck` ratios and mainly test class-boundary stability.

## 5. Reviewer-Risk Notes

The main risk is overclaiming. The add-on should be framed as a decision-gate and hypothesis-reframing analysis. It should not be presented as external empirical validation of the SPINE simulator or as a broad survey of spine-neck resistance.

## 6. How To Answer The "Only Three Rows" Objection

Answer directly: the analysis intentionally uses only the three rows that passed the Codex-only, source-backed summary gate. The purpose is not to estimate prevalence or population variability. The purpose is to test whether the available machine-recoverable summary anchors remain separated under transparent load choices. That narrow test passes, but broader inference remains unsupported.

## 7. How To Answer The "Harnett Is Model/Summary, Not Measured Per-Spine" Objection

Agree with the objection and treat it as a design constraint. The Harnett row must be called a model/summary high-isolation anchor. It should never be described as recovered native paired measured per-spine data. The manuscript add-on can still use it as a contrast anchor if the caption and text preserve that caveat.

## 8. How To Answer The "Common-Load Models Are Arbitrary" Objection

The common loads are not presented as biological distributions. They are deterministic reference choices: recovered source loads, the SPINE reference load, and threshold-relevant diagnostic values. For common-load calculations, the ratio between Harnett and Popovic `rho_L` values equals the ratio of their `R_neck` values, so the value of the exercise is class-boundary stability rather than a new empirical statistic.
