# Phase 2a Manuscript Shape Recommendation

## Recommendation

Use `Shape_A_plus_reframing_hypothesis_only`.

Phase 2a provides enough structure for an optional summary-level empirical overlay or supplement panel, but not enough for a validation claim. The manuscript should frame this as a hypothesis-reframing result: external summary anchors fall into different load-normalized regimes, suggesting that some apparent cross-study disagreement may reflect different effective neck-to-load ratios rather than a single universal spine-neck resistance narrative.

## What The Manuscript May Say

Safe claim shape:

> In a summary-level decision-gate overlay, the Harnett model/proxy high-isolation anchor remains in a high `rho_L` regime, whereas Popovic measured and model-fit anchors remain low or intermediate across source-native and common-reference loads. This supports a cautious load-normalized reframing hypothesis, not a per-spine validation.

Recommended wording elements:

- State that Phase 0.1 permitted `GO_SUMMARY_ONLY`, not `GO_PER_SPINE`.
- Name Harnett as a model/proxy high-isolation anchor.
- Name Popovic measured and model-fit rows separately.
- State that Kwon and Cornejo remain nonnumeric for accepted `rho_L` coordinates.
- State that transfer metrics were audited as proxy-only and no SPINE residuals were computed.
- Keep `rho_L` and `Gamma_div` definitions tied to the existing SMI/divider framing.

## What The Manuscript Must Not Say

Do not claim:

- SPINE is biologically validated by the external dataset.
- The controversy is resolved.
- Harnett native paired measured `R_neck/R_d-EPSC` rows were recovered.
- The overlay is a per-spine empirical sample.
- The Popovic AR proxy is an exact SPINE residual.
- Kwon or Cornejo provide accepted numeric `rho_L` coordinates in the current dataset.
- SMI is a universal transient or nonlinear predictor.

## Figure Placement

The Phase 2a figure is best suited for one of these bounded uses:

- Supplementary figure supporting a short "external summary decision gate" paragraph.
- Main-text schematic/summary figure only if the caption is explicit that this is a summary-level hypothesis-reframing overlay.
- Internal decision memo figure if the manuscript should remain conservative until Phase 2b.

Preferred caption logic:

> Summary-level external anchors were placed on the analytic divider relationship and re-evaluated under four load models. Harnett remains high-isolation, Popovic measured remains low-isolation, and Popovic model-fit remains low or intermediate. Rows are summary/model anchors only; no per-spine inference or exact residual analysis is implied.

## Recommended Manuscript Structure

If included after Phase 2b, use a compact add-on:

1. A short paragraph after the core SMI/divider result explaining why external summary anchors were treated as a decision gate.
2. A methods note describing the locked Phase 1 schema, the four Phase 2a load models, and the no-per-spine/no-digitization rule.
3. A figure or supplement panel using `phase2a_summary_route1_decision_gate`.
4. A limitation sentence that transfer metrics were proxy-only and that Kwon/Cornejo lacked accepted numeric `rho_L` coordinates.

Do not rewrite manuscript equations, definitions, parameter values, or the validated SPINE computational results on the basis of Phase 2a alone.

## Decision

Proceed to `Phase 2b - Bounded Summary-Level Load-Sensitivity and Manuscript Add-On Draft`.

The Phase 2b draft should remain summary-only and should test how much the Phase 2a separation survives modest load/uncertainty perturbations before any manuscript text is finalized.
